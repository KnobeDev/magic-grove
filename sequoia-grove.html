The Grove Has
Been Waiting

There is a grove of Giant Sequoias in the mountains of California that has been watching the world for two thousand years. It is enchanted — not in the fairy-tale sense, but in the older sense: something so alive, so deeply rooted, that it cannot help but pay attention.

The grove has things to share with you. But it is also curious about your life: what you are making, what you are carrying, what you hope survives. Spend about thirty minutes here. When you leave, you will carry a small seed: a digital record of your thinking, structured so any mind, human or machine, can grow with it. Walk among the trees — at each one, the grove asks you something true, and gathers your answers into the seed you carry out.
